Good to know

Clear answers before you install.

Do I need the desktop helper for Google Meet?

No. Google Meet capture runs in the Chrome extension. Install the helper only for calls happening in desktop apps such as Zoom, Teams, or Slack.

Why does my computer show an install warning?

To keep downloads free, current desktop releases are not signed with paid platform certificates. The Mac may block the first open; Windows may show SmartScreen. The platform download card gives the steps. Check the release checksum before installing.

How do desktop app updates work?

The helper checks GitHub once a day and asks before opening the official release page. You download and install the new version yourself; the helper never installs updates in the background.

Will I need an audio driver?

Usually not. Current macOS and Windows capture system audio directly, and Linux uses its PulseAudio/PipeWire audio session. A fallback is shown only when the native route is unavailable.

Should I tell people I’m taking notes?

Yes. Get permission from everyone you record where local law or workplace policy requires it. AI Notetaker shows a consent reminder during setup; that does not replace your responsibility to get consent.

Are these real customer stories?

No. The role cards and sample notes describe example ways people might use meeting notes. They are not reviews or endorsements.
