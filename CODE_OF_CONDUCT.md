# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in this project a harassment-free
experience for everyone, regardless of age, disability, ethnicity, gender
identity or expression, level of experience, nationality, appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contribute to a positive environment include
welcoming and inclusive language, respect for differing viewpoints, accepting
constructive feedback, and focusing on what is best for the community.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, intimidation, personal attacks,
publishing private information, and other conduct that would be inappropriate
in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the project maintainers through the
contact method listed in the repository security policy. Reports will be
reviewed and handled confidentially where possible. Maintainers may remove
comments, reject contributions, or restrict participation when necessary to
protect the community.

This policy is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1.
